# DNV Brand Style Guide

## Overview

This guide outlines the DNV corporate visual identity, including color palettes, typography, and usage guidelines to ensure visual consistency across all materials.

---

## Primary Colour Palette

DNV has seven primary colours: **Sky blue**, **Land green**, **Sea blue**, **Dark blue**, **White**, **Cyan**, and **Black**.

The logo uses the four colours in the top row (Sky blue, Land green, Sea blue, Dark blue), but the primary overall brand colours are **White** and **Dark Blue**.

- **White** references our academic, sage-like approach to business and helps emphasise the importance of well-considered marks on papers and screens
- **Dark Blue** adds weight and contrast to our brand

## Colour Usage Guidelines

### Hierarchy and Balance

**White and Dark Blue play a hero role** in the DNV visual identity.

1. **White**: Background colour; creates sense of space; use generously
2. **Dark Blue**: Adds contrast; breaks up white space; effective for titles and headings
3. **Sea Blue**: Subheading typography
4. **Sky Blue**: Background blocks/fact boxes with good text legibility
5. **Land Green & Cyan**: Use sparingly; primarily for information graphics
6. **Black**: Body copy

### Application Principles

- Be generous with white space
- Dark blue can fill whole pages to add variation in multi-page documents
- Dark blue works effectively for title and heading typography
- Sea blue for subheading typography
- Sky blue for backgrounds where text needs to be easily read
- Cyan should NOT be used as Sky Blue (insufficient contrast)
- Land Green and Cyan feature mainly in information graphics
- Black for body copy

## Use of Secondary Colours

Secondary colours are used for:
- Information graphics (tables, graphs, diagrams)
- Creating categories and chapters in publications (small amounts on tabs/details)
- With the Transformation Graphic on Dark Blue background

### Colour Order for Information Graphics

1. **Start with primary colours** to establish brand presence
2. Follow with solid secondary colours
3. Finally, use specific tints of secondary colours
4. Adjust order after primary colours to ensure sufficient contrast

## Quick Reference

### For Document Design
- **Background**: White (generous use)
- **Titles/Headings**: Dark Blue (#0f204b)
- **Subheadings**: Sea Blue (#003591)
- **Body Text**: Black (#000000)
- **Fact Boxes**: Sky Blue background (#99d9f0)
- **Information Graphics**: Primary colours first, then secondary palette

### For Brand Applications
- **Hero colours**: White + Dark Blue
- **Logo colours**: Sky Blue, Land Green, Sea Blue, Dark Blue
- **Avoid**: Using Cyan as background (use Sky Blue instead)
- **Emphasis**: Be generous with white space
