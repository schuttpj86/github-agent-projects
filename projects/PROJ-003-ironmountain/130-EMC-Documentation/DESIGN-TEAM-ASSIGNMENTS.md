## Action Items for Each Design Team Lead

### Step 1: Review Your Assignments
- Check which buildings you're responsible for (see table above)
- Contact project coordinator if assignments need adjustment
- Identify which team members will assist with data collection

### Step 2: Gather Required Documentation
For each building you're responsible for, collect:

1. **Building Drawings** (Sub-folder 1)
   - Floor plans with equipment locations
   - Elevation views
   - Room layouts showing equipment enclosures

2. **Equipment Lists** (Sub-folder 2)
   - NEW equipment only (DP2-4 project)
   - Equipment name, brand, model number
   - Location within building
   - Excel/CSV format preferred

3. **Equipment Specifications** (Sub-folder 3)
   - Manufacturer datasheets (PDF)
   - EMC specifications
   - Compliance certificates

### Step 3: Upload to SharePoint
- Navigate to your assigned building folder(s)
- Upload documents to the appropriate sub-folder
- Follow naming conventions suggested in UPLOAD_HERE.txt files

### Step 4: Verify Completeness
- Check that all three sub-folders have content
- Ensure equipment in List (Sub-folder 2) matches Specifications (Sub-folder 3)
- Drawings (Sub-folder 1) should show all listed equipment

### Step 5: Notify Coordinator
- Email Pieter H Pretorius when your sections are complete
- Flag any missing information or uncertainties

## Important Notes

### Scope Clarification
- **ONLY** include NEW equipment introduced under **Project DP2-4**
- Do NOT include existing equipment unless being replaced/modified
- If unsure whether equipment is in scope, contact project coordinator

### Multi-Disciplinary Buildings
Some buildings require input from multiple disciplines:
- **Coordinate** with other leads when both are assigned
- **Clearly mark** which equipment is your discipline's responsibility
- **Avoid duplication** - agree who uploads shared equipment specs

### Equipment Examples by Building Type

**CDG & RNB Buildings**: Industrial switches, network equipment, servers, UPS, HVAC controls

**NSA Building**: Generators, fuel systems, control panels, battery banks, switchgear

**Takhuisjes**: Protection relays, control panels, RTUs, communication equipment

**Transformer/Reactor Cells**: Transformers, reactors, cooling systems, bushings, monitoring equipment

**Storage & LV Buildings**: Distribution panels, lighting, building services equipment
