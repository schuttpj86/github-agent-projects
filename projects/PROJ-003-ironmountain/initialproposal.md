# [1

Introduction]()

This proposal is based on the meetings with Iron
Mountain to support new connection requests to Alliander and TenneT.

Iron Mountain is a global information-management
company that helps organisations handle their physical and digital assets
(documents, data, media, etc.) throughout their lifecycle. Services include:

Secure physical storage of records, documents and
media in dedicated facilities

Digitisation of physical materials (scanning
documents, media) and converting to digital assets

Digital information management: data centres, cloud
solutions, data migration, governance and compliance

Iron Mountain has held multiple discussions with
Alliander and TenneT to increase the connection capacity from 30 MVA to
potentially 70 MVA, within the Haarlem region. Due to grid congestion a
connection (enhancement) request can take up multiple years, resulting that
Iron Mountain ended up on the “waiting list”. By solely requesting additional
capacity Iron Mountain is not likely to receive additional capacity, other options
need to be explored to define a feasible approach.

TRC and DNV combined can support to define an edge
in the capacity request, which could help Iron Mountain in moving up the
waiting list and faster realisation.

## [2.1

Understanding the current
capacity request]()

Firstly, the status of the current request towards
Alliander and/or TenneT needs to be clarified (which request to which system
operator, and the status of the request). This ensures having the right
going-in position for further discussions.

Secondly, Iron Mountain shall provide the currently
available information on the connection request(s):

**1.
****Current connection**

a.
Electricity profile

b.
To which feeder/substation connected

**2.
****Envisioned connection**

a.
Envisioned new electricity profile / additional
request

b.
New request to existing feeder/substation Alliander
and/or totally new request to TenneT (feeder/substation)

**3.
****Ongoing (peripheral) discussions**

a.
What has already been discussed with Alliander,
TenneT and 3^rd^ parties?

b.
Potential parallel discussions with windfarms,
industry park neighbours

The information provisioning by Iron Mountain will
be used in task 3 “defining a tailored solution for the capacity request”.

## [2.2

Aligning with the energy hub(s)]()

Within the province of Noord Holland the “taskforce
e-infra Noord Holland” has been established ([https://energieregionh.nl/](https://energieregionh.nl/)). This
taskforce bundles regional initiatives to provide support in connection
requests, energy portfolio optimisation and renewable energy integration.
Aligning with these energy hub(s) can have multiple advantages:

**Optimising the energy portfolio behind the
feeder(s) of the substations**

Aligning the energy profiles of (most) prosumers, which have electrotechnical
connections to each other, to create additional capacity at substation level

**Finding partners to jointly optimise/invest
in the energy portfolio

**Collaboration partners that want to invest in e.g., a battery or wind
farms to create a solution that’s not available with a single request.

We can introduce Iron Mountain to the energy
hub(s), and in parallel define options on how to optimise the energy portfolio.

## [2.3

Defining a tailored solution
for the capacity request]()

The largest challenge is in defining a potential
solution for Alliander/TenneT. Both system operators can’t provide consultancy
services for capacity requests, since all requests need to be treated equally. Solely
requesting a new baseload, creates a new problem for TenneT/Alliander and puts Iron
Mountain at the bottom of the waiting list.

Defining a tailored solution, based on the local
grid constraints, can provide an edge in the request. When Iron Mountain can
support the system operators with particular issues (that create grid
congestion / block the request) a solution can be created. Depending on the
local constraints these could be e.g., mitigating voltage quality issues,
frequency instability issues and/or peak capacity shaping. It could still be
possible that a capacity request can’t be fulfilled by the system operator(s),
but it largely increases the chances of success.

We offer a proven framework to prioritise
investments and optimise collaboration to all move towards the same goal

![]()

By combining our knowledge in “customer engagement”
(deregulated domain) with the regulated departments of the system operators we
can provide grid calculations and congestion analysis.

We will analyse the local conditions of the power
grid and provide potential solutions specifically for that substation/region.

timetable for the change including any

(d)
impact on the Services to be provided
