### 2 THE DNV PROPOSED SOLUTION

**2.1 Approach**
To meet WEB Aruba’s accelerated 2-to-3-month project schedule for the upcoming EPC tender, DNV proposes a targeted, fast-track engineering assessment. Our approach focuses on delivering actionable pre-engineering specifications while mitigating the highest-risk integration elements. 

Based on our alignment meeting, DNV’s approach will include:
* **Targeted System Studies:** Rather than performing a full dynamic system impact study from scratch, DNV will conduct targeted analytical studies—specifically short-circuit analysis, steady-state load flow, and frequency scan/resonance checks. This ensures the safe specification of the 60 kV equipment, validates the 100% redundancy sizing of the 15 km cables (~90 MW per feeder), and assesses potential switching overvoltages or capacitive charging issues.
* **Optimized Cable Routing:** For the ~15 km cable trajectory, DNV will evaluate the ~10 km section running parallel to existing WEB feeders to assess trench capacity and thermal derating. For the new ~5 km section, DNV will leverage existing environmental and routing data previously gathered during the natural gas pipeline trajectory assessment, saving valuable time and reducing permitting uncertainties.
* **Substation & Transformer Engineering:** DNV will define the integration requirements for the existing GIS breaker at Substation EAST and the new GIS breaker at Substation WEST. Concurrently, our transformer experts will validate the sizing, impedance, and tap-changer requirements for the two 100%-rated 60/?? kV main step-down transformers at the new Rincon substation.

**2.2 Deliverables**
In accordance with the Terms of Reference, DNV will provide the following deliverables:
1. **Technical Feasibility Report**, which will include:
   * High-level system assessment (Short-Circuit, Load Flow, and Resonance checks).
   * Cable route assessment and thermal bottleneck evaluation.
   * Transformer redundancy validation and sizing recommendations.
   * Comprehensive Risk Register (covering technical, constructability, operational, and permitting risks) with proposed mitigation measures.
2. **Preliminary Single-Line Diagram (SLD)** clearly marking the Point of Common Coupling (POCC), protection zones, and equipment ratings.
3. **Pre-Engineering Scope Definition**, structurally formatted for WEB Aruba's immediate use as the technical basis for an EPC tender.
4. **Budgetary Estimate** providing an indicative Class 4/5 CAPEX range for the BOP infrastructure (equipment and installation).

**2.3 Alternative and/or optional work**
Should this be of interest, DNV would be pleased to amend the present proposal accordingly, or submit a separate proposal for the following items:
* **Site Visit & Physical Verification:** While this base proposal is a desktop-based study, DNV strongly recommends adding a 3-to-4-day site visit by a DNV cable routing engineer and substation expert. This visit would physically verify trench capacities along the ~10 km existing route and confirm spatial civil constraints for the new GIS breaker at Substation WEST.
* **Dynamic Grid Stability Studies:** Transient stability and dynamic grid code compliance studies are currently excluded from this BOP pre-engineering scope. If WEB Aruba requires updated dynamic models for the island grid integrating the new 90 MW capacity, DNV can provide this as an optional addition.
* **Geotechnical / Soil Thermal Resistivity Testing:** Physical soil testing is excluded from this fast-track phase to save time but can be offered as a subsequent scope to refine cable thermal ratings.
