### 3 SCOPE OF WORK

To deliver the proposed solution, DNV has structured the Scope of Work into five key sequential tasks:

**Task 1: Project Initiation, Data Gathering & Basis of Design (BoD)**
* **1.1 Kick-off & Alignment:** Conduct a project kick-off meeting with WEB Aruba to finalize communication protocols, project timelines, and confirm the exact Point of Common Coupling (POCC) boundaries.
* **1.2 Data Review:** Review all existing documentation provided by WEB Aruba, including 60 kV network SLDs, GIS switchgear OEM manuals (for Substations EAST & WEST), as-built trench data for the existing ~10 km cable route, and any preliminary data from the wind developer.
* **1.3 Basis of Design (BoD):** Develop a concise BoD document defining the electrical parameters, N-1 redundancy criteria, environmental conditions, and regulatory/design standards that will govern the pre-engineering phase.

**Task 2: Targeted Power System Studies**
* **2.1 Steady-State Load Flow & Short-Circuit Assessment:** Perform analytical calculations to evaluate the integration of the 90 MW capacity into the 60 kV system. This will validate bulk power transfer capabilities, confirm voltage profiles, and assess the fault level impact on the existing 60 kV GIS switchgear.
* **2.2 Resonance & Energization Check (Frequency Scan):** Given the significant capacitance of dual 15 km, 60 kV underground cables and the large inductance of the 90 MVA transformers, DNV will perform a frequency scan to identify potential harmonic resonance issues, capacitive charging current limits, and switching overvoltage risks during energization.

**Task 3: Cable System Sizing & Routing Assessment**
* **3.1 Cable Sizing:** Perform preliminary continuous current rating and short-circuit sizing calculations for the 60 kV underground cables in accordance with IEC 60287. Cables will be sized to carry 100% of the combined wind park capacity (~90 MW) to meet strict N-1 redundancy requirements.
* **3.2 Thermal Bottleneck Analysis (~10 km existing route):** Evaluate the thermal interaction risks and potential derating for the ~10 km section where the new cables will run parallel to existing WEB feeders.
* **3.3 Desktop Route Assessment (~5 km new route):** Execute a desktop-based route assessment for the new trajectory leading to the Rincon substation. DNV will heavily leverage historical environmental, permitting, and routing data from the previously proposed natural gas pipeline project to accelerate this assessment and identify major obstacles or trenching constraints.

**Task 4: Substation Integration & Transformer Specification**
* **4.1 Transformer Specification:** Develop the preliminary technical specifications for the two 60/?? kV main step-down transformers at the Rincon substation. This includes validating the 100% capacity rating (~90 MVA each), defining impedance, vector group, cooling (ONAN/ONAF), grounding, and evaluating the necessity of an On-Load Tap Changer (OLTC) versus the requested No-Load Tap Changer (NLTC).
* **4.2 Substation EAST Integration:** Evaluate the technical capability, control interfaces, and any required modifications to terminate Feeder 1 into the existing 60 kV GIS breaker (STG#3).
* **4.3 Substation WEST Integration:** Define the technical specifications, physical space requirements, and interlocking/protection logic for integrating a completely new 60 kV GIS breaker for Feeder 2.

**Task 5: Risk Assessment, CAPEX Estimation & Pre-Engineering Scope**
* **5.1 Risk Register:** Develop a comprehensive Risk Register identifying technical, constructability, operational, and environmental/permitting risks associated with the BOP infrastructure. DNV will propose specific mitigation measures for each identified risk.
* **5.2 CAPEX Estimation:** Develop an indicative Class 4/5 budgetary estimate (AACE International standard: approx. -30% to +50% accuracy) covering the anticipated equipment, cable supply, and installation costs.
* **5.3 EPC Tender Scope Definition:** Compile the findings from Tasks 1-4 into a clearly structured, functional Pre-Engineering Scope document, accompanied by a Preliminary Single-Line Diagram (SLD). This package will be formatted specifically for WEB Aruba to use as the technical foundation for their EPC tendering process.
