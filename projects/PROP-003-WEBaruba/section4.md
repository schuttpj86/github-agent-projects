### 4 SCHEDULE

To align with WEB Aruba’s accelerated requirements for the upcoming EPC tender, DNV proposes a fast-track execution schedule of **10 weeks** from the receipt of the formal Notice to Proceed (NTP) and the required base data. 

The estimated schedule and key milestones are outlined below:

* **Week 1:** Project Kick-off Meeting & Initial Data Transfer
* **Week 3:** Delivery of Basis of Design (BoD) for WEB Aruba review
* **Week 6:** Completion of Targeted Grid Studies & Cable Sizing calculations
* **Week 8:** Delivery of Draft Technical Feasibility Report & Preliminary SLD 
* **Week 10:** Delivery of Final Report, Pre-Engineering Scope Document, Risk Register, and Class 4/5 CAPEX Estimate

*Note: This schedule assumes timely provision of necessary grid and substation data from WEB Aruba, as well as prompt feedback (within 1 week) on draft deliverables.*
