**7.2 Assumptions, conditions and limitations**
In addition to possible assumptions, conditions and limitations stated elsewhere, this proposal is issued on the basis of the following assumptions with respect to deliverables, scope of work, schedule and costs:

1. **Grid Data Provision:** WEB Aruba will provide existing, up-to-date power system data (e.g., SLDs, existing switchgear OEM manuals, and basic load flow/short circuit base cases) necessary to perform the targeted analytical studies within the first week of the project.
2. **Exclusion of Full Dynamic Studies:** Full dynamic stability, transient stability, and detailed grid code compliance studies are excluded from this fast-track BOP pre-engineering scope. DNV’s electrical assessment will be limited to steady-state load flow, short circuit, and frequency scan/resonance checks to size equipment and identify energization risks.
3. **Wind Developer Inputs:** It is assumed the wind developer will provide their required collector bus voltage (the "?? kV" side of the main transformers) and basic P/Q operational capabilities within the first two weeks of the project. If delayed, DNV will proceed using a standard assumed voltage (e.g., 34.5 kV) to maintain the schedule.
4. **Substation WEST Civil Space:** It is assumed that there is adequate physical space inside the existing Substation WEST building to install the new 60 kV GIS breaker bay without requiring major structural extensions.
5. **Route Data Availability:** For the desktop cable routing assessment, DNV will rely on historical soil data and existing environmental/routing data from the previously proposed natural gas pipeline project. No new physical geotechnical investigations or GPR scans are included in this phase.
