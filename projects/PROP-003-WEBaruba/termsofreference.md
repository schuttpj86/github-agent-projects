Terms of Reference (TOR) 

Subject: Technical Feasibility Assessment – Balance of Plant (BOP) for future wind energy integration – WEB Aruba 

Updated: February 25, 2026 

Background 

WEB Aruba is evaluating the integration of two future wind projects: 

WP-Rincon (new wind park) 

WP-Vader Piet 2 (repowering of existing WP-Vaderpiet – currently 30MW) 

The estimated combined installed capacity is approximately 90 MW (~45 MW each) and is subject to ongoing feasibility confirmation by the wind project developer. 

The Balance of Plant (BOP) infrastructure required to connect these wind parks to WEB’s 60kV system will fall within WEB’s scope. An engineering consultant is requested to support WEB Aruba with a technical feasibility assessment and development of a pre-engineering scope. 

Objective 

The objective of this assignment is to: 

Define the technical requirements for the proposed BOP. 

Identify system constraints and required mitigation measures. 

Assess technical and operational risks. 

Evaluate feasible configuration alternatives. 

Support development of a pre-engineering design scope. 

Provide a budgetary estimate for the pre-engineering phase. 

Description (high-level concept) 

Grid Connection Concept 

Two approximately 15 km, 60 kV feeder cables will connect WEB Aruba’s 60 kV Substations (EAST & WEST) to step-down transformers located near the wind parks. 

The BOP under WEB’s scope extends up to the high-voltage side of the 60 kV/?? kV step-down transformers. 

All BOP equipment on the low-voltage side falls under the wind developer’s scope. 

The Point of Common Coupling (POCC) will be located at the wind park’s central substation. 

Substation interface 

Feeder 1: Connected to an existing 60 kV GIS breaker at Substation EAST. 

Feeder 2: Connected to a new 60 kV GIS breaker at Substation WEST. 

The new GIS breaker should be engineered, supplied, installed, and integrated into the existing 60 kV switchgear.

Assumptions (preliminary) 

Total installed wind capacity: ~90 MW (subject to confirmation). 

Estimated feeder length: ~15 km (Google Earth). 

Transformer basic requirements: 

Voltage rating: HV: 60 kV | LV: ?? kV (to be confirmed based on wind developer design) 

Vector group: YnD1 

Cooling: ONAN / ONAF 

Tap changer: NLTC, ±2.5% total range (final tap range to be confirmed) 

Frequency: 60 Hz 

System grounding: Solidly grounded 60 kV 

Impedance: Preliminary range: 8–12% (to be validated) 

Short-circuit withstand capability: In accordance with IEC 60076 and match maximum fault level at 60 kV bus. 

Rating philosophy: 

Each transformer shall be rated for 100% of the total combined wind park capacity (~90 MW estimated) 

Operational requirement: 

One transformer may be taken out of service without interruption of total wind production.
